Questions

Before you get started.

Who is Alderly for?

Alderly is designed for older adults who need recurring assistance and for family members who want a trusted way to coordinate that care, especially when they cannot manage everything themselves.

Does Alderly provide medical treatment?

No. Alderly coordinates services and logistics. Medical diagnosis and treatment remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals and hospitals.

Can family members living outside Gorakhpur use it?

Yes. The experience is designed around family coordination, so a designated family member can stay informed about requests, appointments and recurring care activities.

Can I request a nurse or caretaker for a few hours?

The launch model can support recurring and scheduled home-care requests, subject to partner availability and the specific care requirement.

Is Alderly an emergency service?

No. Alderly is not a replacement for emergency medical services. In an emergency, families should contact the appropriate emergency medical service or hospital immediately.
